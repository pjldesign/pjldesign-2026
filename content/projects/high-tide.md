<p>Illustration in collaboration with <a href="https://hightidenyc.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">High Tide NYC</a> for a limited-edition quarantine 'Zine'. Worked with Danny Miller, High Tide AD and Richard Dryden aka <a href="https://www.instagram.com/treatise/?hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">DJ Treats</a>, famed DJ and all-around NYC nightlife impressario to concieve and illustrate an article about Richard's bizarrely abrupt, COVID-19-prompted pilgramage (with family in tow) from New York City &mdash; our mutual native home &mdash; to unfamiliar Texas, and the resulting feelings of displacement, longing, shock and FOMO. Illustration in gouache and watercolor.</p>
