<p class="p1">Title sequence and top level typographic design direction for Spike Television and Jay-Z produced, MTV Movie Award winning (Best Documentary) documentary series, &ldquo;TIME: The Kalief Browder Story&rdquo;. In six parts, "TIME" tells the story of a 16-year-old student from the Bronx who spent three years on Rikers Island without ever being convicted of a crime and the ways in which his story addresses the American criminal-justice system.</p>
