<p>The Seeing Place is an actor-driven, off-Broadway theater company located in the East Village. Led collaboratively by a multi-award winning ensemble, their primary focus is on social justice theater, with many of the procuctions centering on stories by and about women, immigrants, LGBTQIA+, Global Majority, and people with disabilities/chronic illnesses. I worked with the company to recreate their identity.</p>
