<p>Cover designs and illustration for Money, Blood and Conscience by David Steinman. David is a 2019 Nobel Peace Prize nominee and life-long freedom-fighter who was a consulting expert to the U.S. National Security Council, coordinated private support for Southeast Asian anti-Communist resistance movements and served as senior foreign advisor to Ethiopia's democracy movement for 27 years, co-planning the 2005 civil resistance and electoral campaign for which the novel is based.</p>
