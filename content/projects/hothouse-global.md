<p>Art Direction, Branding Strategy, Digital Animation and Illustration for Hothouse Global, an extension of Hothouse necessitated and inspired by the pandemic. Hothouse Global is a Twitch channel that sponsors and broadcasts streaming concerts with musicians from all corners of the globe, from hometown Chicago to Egypt to Japan. In aid of the Concert for Cuba, an momentous and hugely successful livestream broadcast featuring speakers like Michael Moore, Jesse Jackson and Danny Glover and performers like Michael McDonald, Tom Morello and The Chicago Philharmonic that recieved millions of streaming concert viewers, I provided Art Direction for the broadcast as well as animated interstitials for various scheduled segments of the stream.</p>
