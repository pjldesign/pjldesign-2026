<p>Art direction, motion graphics and design of all promotional materials for Blowback Productions' award-winning feature documentary, "Captured" which through footage captured by its chronicler Clayton Patterson from 1977 to 2008, documents the final era of raw creativity and lawlessness in New York City's Lower East Side.</p>
