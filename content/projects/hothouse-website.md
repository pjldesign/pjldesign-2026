<p>Front-end redesign and Wordpress development of HOTHOUSE Chicago's digital platform.</p>
<p>HotHouse is a 30 year old Arts and Culture landmark in Chicago that was an unfortunate casualty of the millennial recession. It is now building a new site for its multi-disciplinary programs in Chicago. The concept includes a performing arts center, restaurant, green space, artists in residence space and other elements to create a "sanctuary for progressive culture".</p>
<p>In advance of being considered for a multi-million dollar grant from the City of Chicago for this development, HOTHOUSE needed to revolutionize both their digital and branding platforms in a way that would illuminate their history, culture and current and upcoming events and projects as well as project a progressive image towards a burgeoning, vibrant youth demo. Happily, HOTHOUSE has now been admitted to the second round of the grant process with a hopeful award in the offing.</p>
